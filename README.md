# Puzzle Pals

A cozy jigsaw puzzle game for kids: 122 storybook puzzles across 12 worlds, with Gracie, a friendly recorded voice who cheers, counts and reads along.

**Version 1.1**

## What's inside

- Puzzles in 4, 9, 16 or 25 pieces, with an "Easy snap" setting for little fingers
- Picture, ABC or 123 pieces: letters or numbers on each piece and on the board to match
- After each puzzle:
  - Find it!
  - Color it!
  - Spell it! (tap the letters to spell a word from the picture)
  - Count it! (tap each thing and count along)
  - Story (Gracie reads a short story and each word lights up)
- Daily puzzle with a streak, 20 collectible pals, a sticker book, and puzzles from your own photos
- 18 songs, including one for each world, and 9 animated backgrounds

## Play

Open the GitHub Pages site for this repository. Puzzle Pals can be installed like an app: on iPhone or iPad tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen"; on Android or desktop Chrome use "Install app". Under the voice settings, "Save everything for offline play" downloads the whole game so it works without internet.

Progress (stars, stickers, chosen music, background and volume) is saved in each player's own browser.
